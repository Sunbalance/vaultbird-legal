VaultBird: Document Safe: Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

VaultBird is an app for keeping documents, photos and files in an encrypted safe on your iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. VaultBird has no account, no sign-in and no VaultBird server. The app does not send your documents, your passcode, your keys or any usage data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you add is encrypted on your iPhone and stored only on your iPhone. Because we never receive it, we cannot read it, recover it, reset your passcode or restore your safe for you.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan a document or take a photo directly into your safe. Images go straight into the encrypted safe.
  • Face ID: to release a secret held by your iPhone that is one of the factors that opens the safe. Face ID data stays inside iOS; VaultBird never sees it.
  • NFC: only if you choose to use a physical security key, to talk to that key.
  • Photos and Files: only the items you choose to import.

Backups

If you back up to a USB-C drive, VaultBird copies your already-encrypted safe to the drive you pick. The copy opens only with your recovery key. It never leaves your control and is not sent anywhere by the app.

Purchases

VaultBird Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and handles your purchase information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device only to unlock Premium.

Sharing and exporting

When you use Share or Export on a document, VaultBird creates a temporary unencrypted copy so iOS can pass it to the app you choose, and removes it when the share sheet closes. What that other app does with the file is governed by that app, not by VaultBird.

Children

VaultBird does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security limits

Your safe is protected with AES-256-GCM encryption under keys derived from your passcode, a secret held by your iPhone and, optionally, a hardware key. The implementation has not been independently audited. If you lose both your passcode and your recovery key, nobody, including us, can open your safe.
