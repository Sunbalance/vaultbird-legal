Common questions

I forgot my passcode. Use your recovery key, shown once when you created the safe. VaultBird has no accounts or servers, so we cannot reset a passcode or recover a safe. If you have lost both your passcode and your recovery key, the safe cannot be opened.

Face ID changed on my iPhone. Open the safe with your recovery key, then set a new passcode.

How do I back up? In the app, go to Protection, then Backup, and choose a USB-C drive. The backup is already encrypted and opens only with your recovery key.

Restore Premium on a new iPhone. Open Premium in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Cancel the yearly subscription. Manage it in your Apple account: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
